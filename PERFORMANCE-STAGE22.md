# Stage 22: material demand admission
Opt in with VICTORY_MATERIAL_TEXTURE_DEMAND=1 and VICTORY_DEMAND_ADMISSION=1. Previously material hints only reordered the selected 4096 entries; valid requests outside that selection were ignored. New admission checks current write, recovery flag and due status under the queue lock, interleaves priority with normal work, and preserves the bounded selection. No change to GPU fences or texture contents. Added queue regression test; not compiled or run. No GPU detile or FPS claim. Material observations have their existing readback overhead; test on hardware.
